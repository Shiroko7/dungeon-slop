# M1.3 review — reconcile map geometry after edits

## Outcome

Manual edits now have one reconciliation contract. A committed stroke updates the
grid, room footprints and bounds, feature ownership, corridor paths/endpoints,
room connections, reachability warnings, and layout report together. Undo and
redo restore the matching room-note map as well as geometry.

## Review focus

- Edited rooms retain their procedural shape until their cells change; edited
  rooms render and hit-test from their canonical cell footprint.
- Room splits keep the original ID on the component containing the old anchor.
  New components receive fresh IDs without copied role, plan, tier, or narrative.
  Deleted room notes stay recoverable in the edit history and are restored by undo.
- Corridor connections are derived from live traversable cells. Disconnected
  rooms remain visible and are called out in the Layout panel instead of being
  silently reconnected.
- Feature records are deduplicated by cell and removed when a wall/erase stroke
  removes their owning cell. Door, stair, trap, and treasure edits therefore
  round-trip through renderer/export inputs without ghost records.
- Fast pointer samples are interpolated with Bresenham, and each pointer stroke
  remains one undoable operation.
- Unresolved manual corridor endpoints use the persisted `-1` dead-end sentinel.
